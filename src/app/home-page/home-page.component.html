YEAR 2025 IN REVIEW

Thank you for your Visit

We truly appreciate your interest and hope you find the information helpful and engaging. Our club was built on simple but powerful values — supporting one another, strengthening social connections, and contributing to the welfare of others. These values continue to guide every step we take. Over the past year, several moments reminded us of the importance of standing together. In early May, we shared in the joy of a Sister and a Brother's during their child dedication. In June, during a time of loss at a Brother's home, the club offered comfort and strength. July brought opportunities to connect with other groups, attend special events, and support members during personal milestones. August gave us one of our most memorable days — our first BBQ in Karlsruhe Durlach, filled with laughter and togetherness. And throughout September and October, the club continued to show care, respect, and unity at various ceremonies and celebrations. These experiences highlight what makes this club unique: a genuine commitment to one another and a spirit of togetherness that never wavers.

Looking ahead the coming months hold promising opportunities for growth and impact.
